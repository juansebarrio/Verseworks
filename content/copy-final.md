## EXPERIENCES (/experiences — Live Experiences)

### Hero

Poetry becomes an experience when someone enters it.
Verseworks creates encounters that can be heard, held, entered, contributed to and
taken home. Our work spans three areas: live experiences, participatory programmes
and creative commissions.

### Poems made live

A live poem can begin with a thought, memory, object, feeling, question or prompt.
The response may be written, spoken, performed, projected or created digitally —
for one person or with an entire room.

Possible formats include:

- Spoken or performed aloud
- Projected or displayed digitally
- Printed or produced as an object
- Typed or written live, including by typewriter or calligraphy where the format
  suits the context
- Created in one or several languages

Every exchange is different because every participant is different.

### Poetry performances

Commissioned performances, readings and spoken-word interventions created for
openings, dinners, festivals, launches and cultural programmes.

### Poetic hosting

Poets, writers and skilled moderators who can welcome an audience, introduce an
occasion or guide a conversation with warmth and originality.

### Poetry that remains

Some experiences leave a physical trace: a poem, publication, printed work,
edition or object. The form follows the project rather than defining it.

A room, neighbourhood or event contributes to a single evolving poem.

shared question. Verseworks then shapes the contributions into a collective work.

## FOR HOTELS AND BRANDS (/hotels-and-brands — landing fuera de nav)

### Hero

Poetry, made for the room.
Verseworks creates live, multilingual poetry experiences for hotels, hospitality
spaces and brands — from intimate encounters to performances, installations and
property-wide programmes. Each project is shaped around the host, setting and
audience, while remaining artist-led and unmistakably alive.

### For hotels

Verseworks can inhabit a lobby, lounge, restaurant, terrace, guest room or entire
property through live writing, performance, installation, conversation and
participatory work. Guests might encounter a poet in conversation, hear a
performance, contribute to a collective work, discover poetry throughout the
property or leave with a piece created during the experience.

Experiences can be created for: Guest cultural programming · Hotel openings · Art
and design weeks · Residencies · Private dinners · Weddings and celebrations ·
Retreats · Valentine's Day · Mother's Day · Ramadan and Eid · Festive programming ·
Significant local occasions

The experience may last one evening, return regularly or become part of a wider
residency.

### For brands

Verseworks creates participatory poetry experiences in which the audience does
more than look. They might contribute a memory, choose a word, tell a story,
enter into conversation, add to a collective work or encounter poetry in an
unexpected form.

Experiences can be developed for: Product and collection launches · Fashion,
fragrance and beauty events · Retail activations · Client and VIP experiences ·
Campaigns · Anniversaries · Exhibitions · Creative dinners · Conferences · Press
and influencer events

Poetry can appear across paper, packaging, labels, screens, sound, performance,
projection, spatial installations or designed objects — according to the concept.
The branding can be visible, subtle or almost absent. The encounter should always
feel genuine.

### Why poetry?

It creates connection — Poetry creates a human point of contact — between
strangers, audiences, artists, places and ideas.
It is participatory — Guests become part of the experience rather than watching
from a distance.
It is memorable — A poem can be fleeting or monumental, but the encounter often
remains long after the event ends.
It travels — The experience can work across languages, cultures and locations.
It creates natural stories — People photograph, share and speak about what
happened because it mattered to them — not because they were instructed to post
it.

### Designed around the host

Verseworks can create one intimate live encounter or produce a multilingual
poetry programme across an entire property, festival or event.

We can support: Creative concept development · Poet and performer selection ·
Multilingual artist curation · Event format and guest journey · Spatial, printed
and digital formats · Design and production of publications, editions and poetic
objects · On-site management · Documentation · Post-event publications or editions

## ABOUT (/about)

### Hero

Poetry is not only something we read.
It can happen between strangers. It can enter a room. It can become an object, a
performance, a question or a way of gathering people together.

### About Verseworks

Verseworks is a multilingual poetry studio creating participatory experiences,
performances, installations and commissions for festivals, cultural institutions,
public spaces, hospitality and brands.

We work with poets, writers, performers, artists and designers to bring language
into public and shared spaces.

Our projects range from performances, participatory programmes and public art to
installations, multilingual commissions and poems created live with individual
participants. Whatever the scale, the purpose is the same: to create a meaningful
encounter through words.

### Our approach

Personalized — Each project is shaped around its audience, setting and cultural
context.
Participatory — We create ways for people to enter the work rather than only
observe it.
Multilingual by design — Language is considered from the beginning, not added as
an afterthought.
Artist-led and professionally produced — We respect the integrity of the artists
while making the commissioning process clear and dependable for clients.
Made to remain — Even temporary experiences can leave behind a poem, publication,
memory or changed relationship to a place.

### The people behind the poems

Verseworks brings together poets and creative collaborators according to the
needs of each commission.

Our network includes: Page poets · Spoken-word artists · Multilingual writers ·
Performers · Workshop facilitators · Moderators · Visual artists · Designers ·
Creative producers

We select collaborators for the context rather than applying one fixed roster to
every project.

### Founder

Wided Rihana Khadraoui

Verseworks was founded by Wided Rihana Khadraoui, a published poet, cultural
strategist and producer working across contemporary art, cultural institutions
and international events. Her work brings together artistic practice, audience
experience and cultural production. Verseworks grew from the idea that poetry can
operate not only as literature or performance, but as a live medium for
participation, something people can enter, shape and encounter together.
